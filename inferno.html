Inferno: a browser FPS built from scratch, used as a hiring differentiator

Inferno demonstrates systems-level frontend skill: rendering, interaction design, and gameplay UI integrated directly in code.

Enter Orbit to play Inferno Discuss frontend or UE5 UI work

What Inferno includes

  • Raycast walls and complete Canvas-rendered gameplay (no game engine).
  • Six weapons: pistol, shotgun, plasma, BFG, chainsaw, and rockets.
  • Nineteen enemy types, minibosses, keycards, crushers, hazards, teleporters, lifts, and breakable walls.
  • Daily challenge mode, co-op wraith ally mode, and a built-in level editor.
  • Five themed levels and ending cinematic flow.

Why this matters for clients and teams

Inferno proves practical game-feel and UI engineering ability: input handling, performance-minded rendering, feedback loops, and polished interaction under constraints.

That same craft carries into production frontend and UE5 HUD work.
